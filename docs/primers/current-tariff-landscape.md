## Part 1: Status as of 2026-10-08

### 1.0 How to read the Part 1 tables

Status words:

- **In force:** being collected on entries now.
- **Ended:** no longer collected (may still matter for refunds, drawback or audits of past entries).
- **Suspended:** switched off until a stated date.
- **Announced:** stated by the government but not yet in a legal instrument.
- **Pending:** under investigation; no duty yet.
- **Unknown:** not established for this edition.

In this edition the "Status as of 2026-10-08" column gives the **last known status** (about mid-2026) unless it says Unknown. Dates are written YYYY-MM-DD.

Confidence labels (use these exactly when you update a row):

- **Confirmed:** the updater opened the primary source on the date stated and it says this.
- **Reported:** known from an announcement, secondary report or the compiler's knowledge; the primary source is named but was not opened for this edition. Treat it as a lead.
- **Unclear:** details uncertain, sources conflict, or the status has probably changed since mid-2026.

#### US-8 Stacking: last known CBP practice

- **Default:** additional duties add to the base rate and to each other, unless the instrument says otherwise. Every instrument has to be read for its own "does not apply to" or interaction paragraph.
- **2025 non-stacking rules:** EO 14289 (2025-04-29) set rules so that the auto and auto parts Section 232 duties were not stacked with the Canada and Mexico IEEPA duties or the steel and aluminum Section 232 duties, and the Canada and Mexico IEEPA duties were not stacked with the steel and aluminum Section 232 duties (reported). With IEEPA duties ended, the live questions are Section 232 against Section 232 (autos, trucks, metals, copper, wood products) and Section 301 on top of Section 232.
- **Section 301 on China** has applied in addition to Section 232 duties since 2018 (reported).
- **Content-based Section 232 duties:** CBP has required the metal content and the non-metal content to be reported on separate entry lines, with other duties possible on the non-metal line (reported). Check the latest CSMS message for each action.
- **Reporting order:** CBP publishes the order in which Chapter 99 numbers are reported in CSMS messages. The current stacking and reporting-order message was not identified for this edition (V4). Record its number here when found.

Durable point for IMMEX (Reported): under the USMCA's rules on drawback and duty deferral programs (Chapter 2, Article 2.5; text at https://ustr.gov/trade-agreements/free-trade-agreements/united-states-mexico-canada-agreement/agreement-between), Mexico limits the duty it waives on non-originating inputs in goods exported to the US or Canada. ### 1.5 USMCA, CUSMA, T-MEC: the 2026 joint review

What the agreement says (durable, Article 34.7, https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/agreements/FTA/USMCA/Text/34_Final_Provisions.pdf):

- The agreement entered into force on 2020-07-01 for a 16-year term (to 2036-07-01).
- The Free Trade Commission holds a joint review on the sixth anniversary, 2026-07-01. Each party confirms in writing whether it wants to extend the term for another 16 years.
- If all three confirm, the term is extended to 2042 and the next joint review is due within six years.
- If any party does not confirm, the agreement stays in force and the parties hold annual joint reviews for the rest of the term. Any later review can still confirm the extension. Without it, the agreement ends on 2036-07-01.

## Part 2: How to research a new tariff action

### 2.1 What you produce, and the boundary

- **Research owns the WHAT:** the legal authority, scope (HTS, Chapter 99, countries, product language), rates, effective dates, exclusions, stacking, USMCA treatment and sources. You deliver it in a Tariff-Impact Brief (`docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-brief.md`) and the Action tab of the shared Tariff-Impact Model (`docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-model-guide.md`).
- **Standard Cost owns the $:** landed cost, margin, the Oracle cost update and variance explanations. If anyone asks you "how much will this cost us?", the answer is: "Standard Cost will model it from brief v1.0. I can tell you which items, from when and at what rate."
- **Maker-checker:** no brief is released without a documented check by the Senior or the Manager. This continues after day 90.
- **No legal advice:** you record "our working interpretation" with a confidence level. Anything uncertain that changes scope, rate or date goes to the broker or trade counsel before release.

### 2.3 How a US action shows up on an entry

- HTSUS Chapter 99 holds temporary modifications. Subchapter III (headings 9903) holds the additional duties under trade laws such as Sections 232, 301, 201 and 122. Subchapter II (9902) holds temporary duty reductions, which are a different thing.
- A Chapter 99 number is reported **in addition to** the Chapter 1 to 97 classification, never instead of it. It tells CBP which action applies (or which exemption or exclusion is claimed) and therefore which extra rate to charge. CBP's CSMS guidance gives the reporting order.
- Some Chapter 99 numbers mean "not subject" (for example the non-metal content of a derivative, or an excluded product). Reporting them is how the exemption is claimed.
- Long-standing examples, to recognise in entry data (always check the current HTSUS): 9903.80 and 9903.81 for steel, 9903.85 for aluminum, 9903.88 for Section 301 on China, 9903.94 for autos and auto parts. Confidence: Reported.

### 2.9 Trainer review rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. A **material error** is anything that would change which items pay, how much they pay or from when: a wrong or unsupported scope, rate, origin rule, effective date or time, USMCA treatment or stacking result. Any $ figure in a brief is also a material error.

| # | Criterion | 2 = Meets | 1 = Partly | 0 = Material error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary sources | Every fact has a primary source with its date; news and alerts used only as leads | One or two facts cite a secondary source but are correct | A fact rests on a secondary source and is wrong, or a key source is missing |
| 2 | Authority and instrument | Statute, instrument, number, date and issuer correct | Minor citation error | Wrong authority or wrong instrument |
| 3 | Timing | Effective date, time, time zone and basis quoted exactly; in-transit and end or step dates stated | Quoted but one secondary date missing | Wrong effective date or time, or basis misread |
| 4 | HTS scope | List taken from the source as text; digit level and Chapter 99 headings correct | Small format slip with no effect on matching | Codes missing or added; wrong heading |
| 5 | Origin and content | Correct origin rule; partial-value base stated where it applies | Rule correct but not explained | Wrong origin rule, or full value used where content applies |
| 6 | Exclusions and exemptions | Each has an identification method and the evidence needed | Identification method vague | Exclusion missed or misapplied |
| 7 | USMCA, stacking, de minimis, drawback | Each answered with a source, or "not applicable" | One answered without a source | Wrong USMCA or stacking answer |
| 8 | Interpretation and confidence | Quote then interpret; honest confidence; Medium or Low items that change scope sent to the broker or counsel | Confidence missing on one item | A guess presented as fact |
| 9 | Item scoping | Query saved; counts add up; borderline items listed | Counts add up but the query is not saved | Counts do not reconcile, or origin taken from the supplier address |
| 10 | Boundary and control | No $; version, change log and Jira link complete; maker checklist done | One control item missing | $ figures or pricing advice in the brief |

How to use it:

- **Outline (A30-6):** score criteria 1 to 4 only. Pass: no 0 and at least 6 of 8, with the sources found within 60 minutes.
- **Full brief (A60-1):** pass means no 0 (no material error) and at least 16 of 20.
- **Proficiency:** two full briefs in a row with no 0 and only light edits support a rating of 3 (Independent) for "find and read primary sources" (A60-6).
- The checker records the scores, findings and decision on the Jira story and in Brief Part 12.
