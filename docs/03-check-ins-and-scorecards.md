# Check-ins and Scorecards

Last updated: 2026-10-08 · Owner: BSA Manager

**Purpose:** The standard formats for every recurring touchpoint in the 90-day program: the weekly Manager 1:1, the Senior's structured sessions and shadowing, the hires' Friday self-report, and the day-30, day-60 and day-90 review scorecards. Also covers how to give feedback, spot early red flags and adjust the plan.

**Who uses it:** BSA Manager and Senior BSA (to run sessions and reviews). Hire A (Research) and Hire B (Standard Cost), to prepare for them.

**When:** Every week from Day 1 (Mon 2026-10-19), and at the milestone reviews on Wed 2026-11-18, Fri 2026-12-18 and Fri 2027-01-15. Milestone criteria come from `docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Section 4 and use the same IDs.

## 2. Question log

Each hire keeps a running "Question Log" page in their own OneNote section, one row per question. If the trackers workbook gets a question-log tab later, move the log there.

| Date | Question | Module | Where I looked first | Answer and source | Turned into |
|---|-------------|---|---------|-------------|---------|
| 2026-10-21 | Example: Who decides the HTS code the broker files? | M1 | Primer, glossary | Example answer: the team maintains classification in [COMPANY: item master]; the broker files what we provide (Senior, 10/21) | Anki card; SOP line |

Rules:

- **Look first for 15 minutes:** primers, glossary, OneNote, the practice pack. Then log the question, then ask.
- **Blocking questions:** ask right away (Teams message to the Senior or Manager). Log afterwards.
- **Non-blocking questions:** batch them and bring them to the next Senior session or 1:1.
- **Reusable answers:** an answer that someone else will need again becomes an Anki card, a glossary entry or an SOP line within 2 working days. Record which one in the "Turned into" column.
- **Unanswered questions:** any question unanswered after 2 working days is flagged in the Friday self-report.
- **Brokers and counsel:** before the day-60 review, never ask a customs broker, agente aduanal or trade counsel yourself. The question goes to the Senior (or the Manager). After day 60, with the Senior's agreement, you may email the broker with the Senior copied.

## 4. Senior structured session (90 minutes, both hires)

The Senior runs two sessions a week for both hires together, in the standard-week slots. Topics come from the roadmap (`docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Sections 3 and 5). The template keeps prep under 15 minutes and makes sure every session produces something.

### Template

| Time | Block | Senior does | Hires do |
|---|---|-----------------|-----------------|
| Before | Prep | Up to 15 min: pick one real example (an entry, a tariff notice, a query, a report) and one hands-on task. Reuse the primers and practice pack; don't build slides. | Up to 30 min of pre-work set at the previous session. Bring 1–3 logged questions. |
| 0:00–0:10 | Recap and homework check | Ask each hire one question on last session's topic. Review homework output briefly. | Answer without notes. Show homework. |
| 0:10–0:35 | Teach | 25 min on one concept, explained through the real example. Think aloud: what you look at, why, what would worry you. | Take notes; this week's scribe captures key points for OneNote. |
| 0:35–1:10 | Hands-on | Set a task on real or practice data. Watch, and coach with questions rather than answers. | Do the task in pairs or alone (e.g. classify 3 items, read a notice and list affected HTS codes, write a query, audit 5 broker entry lines). |
| 1:10–1:22 | Check for understanding | Use 2 of the techniques below. Note anything not understood for the trackers. | Explain back; find the error; predict the result. |
| 1:22–1:30 | Homework and wrap | Set homework (up to 2 h of self-study with a named output) and the next session's pre-work. | Write down homework, log open questions. |
| After (within 24 h) | Documentation | Optional: 5-minute check of the scribe's notes | Scribe posts notes to OneNote. Each hire writes 2 or more Anki cards from the session and updates the question log. |

Scribe duty rotates between the hires each session.

### Check-for-understanding techniques

- **Explain back:** "Explain this to Hire B as if they missed the session." The other hire adds what's missing.
- **Predict, then check:** "Before you run the query, what count do you expect, and why?"
- **Find the error:** show a deliberately flawed brief line, classification or query. The hires find and fix it.
- **What next:** "The broker's entry shows a different HTS code than our master data. What do you check first?"
- **Teach the boundary:** "Which part of this is Research's job and which is Standard Cost's?"

### Worked example: W3 joint session "Reading a tariff action"

- **Objective:** After this session, both hires can find the primary sources for a US tariff action and extract scope, HTS codes, countries, rate and effective date, and can say which parts Research hands to Standard Cost.
- **Pre-work (30 min):** skim `docs/primers/current-tariff-landscape.md` and `docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-brief.md`.
- **Teach (25 min):** The Senior opens a recent Federal Register notice and the matching CBP CSMS message. The Senior thinks aloud through the effective-date language, the annex of HTS codes, the Chapter 99 heading the broker reports, and any exclusions.
- **Hands-on (35 min):** Hire A lists in-scope HTS codes, countries, rate and effective date with a source for each. Hire B marks which fields the Tariff-Impact Model needs and what $ questions they raise (items, volumes, current duty).
- **Check for understanding:** "Find the error" on a sample brief line with a wrong effective date, then "Teach the boundary".
- **Homework:** Hire A drafts a Tariff-Impact Brief outline for the action, including the candidate items in scope. Hire B lists the cost inputs the model's Items tab needs for Hire A's candidate items (customs value, annual volume, current duty rate, current standard cost, sell price) and where each comes from in [COMPANY: warehouse cost tables or Oracle extract]. Hire B does not decide which items are in scope.

### Shadowing guidelines

Shadowing is how the hires learn the custom tools, AS400 data and the real Research, Standard Cost, exports and reporting work. It costs the trainer little extra time if done this way.

For the person being shadowed (think aloud):

- Say what you're about to do and why: "I'm pulling the entry lines first because the broker's data is the source of truth for what was declared."
- Say what you're checking: "I compare the HTS code on the entry to our item master. A mismatch here is the most common error."
- Name decision points and stop rules: "If the effective date language is unclear, I stop and check the CSMS message before modeling anything."
- Point out where things break: hard-coded values, manual copy steps, files that must be refreshed first.
- Agree pause points (every 10–15 minutes) for questions. Don't stop mid-step to explain.

For the hire shadowing:

- **Before:** read the relevant SOP or tool doc draft (if any) and write 3 questions.
- **During:** take notes in the shadowing format below. Save questions for the pause points.
- **Within 24 hours:** write it up as SOP lines or tool-doc sections, write 2 or more Anki cards, and update the question log.

Shadowing note format:

- Trigger (what starts this work):
- Inputs and where they come from (system, file, person):
- Steps (numbered):
- Decisions and who makes them:
- Controls and checks (including the maker-checker point):
- Outputs and who uses them:
- Where it breaks or depends on one person:
- Open questions:

**Progression on each process or tool:** watch (proficiency 1), then help (2), then do it with the trainer reviewing every step (2), then do it alone with a checker review before release (3). Record each step-up in the Skills tab with the date and the work item.

## 5. Friday self-report (5 prompts, about 15 minutes)

This is the only self-report format; `docs/05-time-management.md` points here. Post it by 11:30 Friday (last working day in holiday weeks) on your OneNote page "Weekly plans and self-reports" and send the link to the Manager, copy to the Senior. It feeds the Friday afternoon 1:1. Use short bullets with links. Don't polish it.

1. **Done this week, with links:** your top-3 outcomes (done or not, and why), Jira items, docs, queries, cards written (count), teach-back topic. Add a rough split of your hours: guided, self-study, shadowing, real work, documentation, and the learning/doing total against your phase target.
2. **One thing I learned that I could teach:** explain one concept in 2–3 sentences in your own words, and name the source.
3. **Stuck or blocked:** access, data, unclear scope, questions open more than 2 working days. Say what you already tried.
4. **Confidence check:** rate the 2–3 skills you focused on this week on the 0–4 proficiency scale, with one piece of evidence each. Say where you feel less confident than the rating suggests.
5. **Next week:** your top 3 priorities, the one most at risk, and one thing you need from the Manager or Senior.

## 6. Milestone reviews and scorecards

### Milestone rating (1–4)

This is not the 0–4 proficiency scale. It rates whether a milestone criterion is met.

| Rating | Meaning | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---------------|
| 1 | Not met | Evidence missing, or the work has material errors |
| 2 | Partly met | Done with heavy help, or some parts missing, or below the threshold |
| 3 | Met | Meets the criterion as written. Normal reviewer comments only. |
| 4 | Exceeded | Met early, or with clearly higher quality or scope (e.g. the doc is already used by others) |

A "material error" is defined in `docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Section 4. It means an error that would change a business decision.

### Preparation

- **Two working days before (Mon 11/16, Wed 12/16, Wed 1/13):** the hire fills in the Evidence column with links and writes a self-rating in Notes, e.g. "Self: 3".
- **Senior input:** the Senior sends ratings and one line of notes for the criteria they checked (M1, M3, M7, M8, Research briefs).
- **Manager prep (45 min per hire):** open every link, spot-check 10 Anki cards, verify 3 Skills-tab ratings against work products, and pre-fill ratings.
- **Day-30 written knowledge check:** taken Mon 11/16, 45 minutes. 20 questions from the customs primer and Anki decks, assembled by the Manager in W4.

### Decision outcome rules

These match `docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Section 8.

- **On track:** every Must criterion rated 3 or 4. Stretch ratings are recorded but do not change the outcome.
- **Adjust:** no Must criterion rated 1, but one or two Must criteria rated 2. Write up to 3 recovery actions with an owner and a due date within 2 weeks.
- **Extend:** any Must criterion rated 1, or three or more Must criteria rated 2. Re-check only the unmet Must criteria on the re-check date and apply "What gives first".

Re-check dates if extended: day 30 on Wed 2026-12-02, day 60 in the W12 1:1 (Fri 2027-01-08), day 90 on Fri 2027-02-12.

## 7. Feedback guidance

### Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI)

Give feedback in three parts, then agree the next step:

- **Situation:** when and where, specifically.
- **Behavior:** what the person did, observable and without judging intent.
- **Impact:** what it caused or would have caused for the business, the checker or the team.
- **Next step:** what to do next time, agreed together. Ask "How do you see it?" before you suggest anything.

### Examples from this program

- **Corrective, Hire A:** "In Tuesday's checker review of your brief on [tariff action] (S), the effective date came from a law-firm alert, not from the Federal Register notice (B). The alert had the wrong date, so the model would have applied the new duty to shipments it doesn't cover, and Hire B's cost impact would have been overstated (I). For the next brief, cite the Federal Register or CSMS for every date and rate, and use secondary sources only as context. Can you add that to your pre-check list?"
- **Corrective, Hire B:** "In the dry-run cost input file (S), the totals weren't tied back to the source extract before you sent it to me (B). I found items missing from the file. In a live cycle they would have kept their old duty rate in Oracle (I). Before any handoff, compare row counts and totals between source and file, and write the result on the checklist."
- **Positive, teach-back:** "At Friday's teach-back (S), you used a real shipment from our data to show where origin and classification meet (B). Hire B asked two follow-up questions and wrote cards from them. That's the documentation habit we want across the team (I)."
- **Positive, owned tool:** "When the tool run failed on Wednesday (S), you checked the tool doc, captured the error, and escalated after 20 minutes with the log attached (B). I fixed it in 5 minutes because you'd already done the triage (I)."

### Rules

- Give feedback within 2 working days of the event. Corrective feedback is private; positive feedback can be public.
- Give one corrective theme at a time. Write it in the 1:1 notes so progress can be checked.
- Give at least one specific positive SBI per hire per week. Generic praise ("good job") doesn't count.
- Separate learning errors from control breaches. Learning errors are expected; catching them is what maker-checker is for. Releasing a tariff model or cost update without checker sign-off is addressed the same day, every time.
- Ask for feedback on the program at the end of every 1:1 and every milestone review, and act on it visibly.

## 8. Early red flags and how to respond

| Red flag | What it may mean | Response |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| Self-report missing or vague 2 weeks in a row | Overloaded, disengaged, or unsure what counts as progress | A 15-minute conversation the same day. Rebuild the week together with time blocks (`docs/05-time-management.md`). |
| Question log empty for a week | Not asking, or not recording | Ask "What did you look up this week?" Set a norm of at least 1 logged question per day in W1–W5. |
| Many questions open more than 2 days | Trainers are the bottleneck | Triage in the 1:1. Route answers to primers or the glossary. Use one Senior session's recap block for the backlog. |
| Anki reviews skipped 3 or more days, or a large overdue backlog | Time management, or the deck feels irrelevant | Fix a daily 20-minute block. Check that the hire writes their own company cards. Suspend low-value cards rather than giving up. |
| Teach-back reads slides and can't answer "why" | Surface learning | Repeat the topic next week with a real example. Ask "why" questions in the next Senior session. |
| Jira items not moving for 5 or more days | Unclear scope, or stuck and not saying so | Split the ticket. Define "done" in one sentence. Agree a 2-day checkpoint. |
| The same error in 2 or more reviews (e.g. effective-date logic, duty base) | A misconception, not a slip | A 30-minute targeted session. Add a line to the hire's pre-check list and an Anki card. |
| Work released without checker sign-off | Doesn't understand the control, or is under pressure | Stop the release or withdraw it. Explain the reason the same day and record it. A second occurrence is escalated per [COMPANY: HR performance process]. |
| Regularly working well over 40 hours | Overload, or poor prioritization | Cut work in progress to 3 Jira items. Review the self-report hour split. Apply "What gives first". |
| Avoiding one area (data work, regulations, Finance contact) | A skill gap or confidence gap | Pair the hires on it. Schedule a short guided rep. Rate it honestly in the Skills tab. |
| Hire B: numbers that don't tie out, with no explanation | No reconciliation habit | Make the tie-out a hard checklist step. Practice it until it is automatic. |
| Hire A: secondary sources cited as authority | Research method gap | Rule: every date, rate and scope claim cites a primary source. The checker returns any brief that doesn't. |
| Skills ratings rising faster than the work shows | Over-confidence, or misreading the scale | Ask for evidence for each change. Re-read the 0–4 scale together. Rate from work products only. |

## 9. How to adjust the plan

Use the cheapest lever that works. Never fix a problem by going over trainer capacity (Manager 8 h, Senior 4.5 h a week).

1. **Re-sequence within the week:** move a topic earlier or later, e.g. if access is late, swap in shadowing.
2. **Change the learning method:** add a worked example, use a different primer section, or replace reading with a hands-on exercise.
3. **Pair the hires:** the stronger hire on a topic explains it to the other. This costs no trainer time and counts toward teach-back practice.
4. **Rebalance trainer time between hires, inside the cap:** for example, move 1 hour of track coaching from one hire to the other for 2 weeks.
5. **Reduce scope:** apply "What gives first" in `docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Section 7.
6. **Extend a milestone:** only at a milestone review, using the decision rules. Exception: access delays beyond Day 3 move the affected criterion's due date by the same number of working days, without waiting for a review.

Rules for any change:

- Record changes to dates, scope or criteria in the plan change log (`docs/01-30-60-90-plan.md` Section 10) the same week.
- Tell both hires and the Senior at the next touchpoint, and update the trackers workbook targets.
- The hires can propose changes in prompt 5 of their Friday self-report. The Manager decides.
