# 30-60-90 Day Plan: BSA New Hires

Last updated: 2026-10-08 · Owner: BSA Manager

**Purpose:** The master plan for the first 90 days of two early-career Business Systems Analysts (BSAs). It covers goals, week-by-week roadmap, milestone exit criteria, trainer load, transitions, risks and milestone decisions.

**Who uses it:** BSA Manager (owner), Senior BSA (co-trainer), Hire A (Research track), Hire B (Standard Cost track).

**When:** Read in full before Day 1 (Mon 2026-10-19). Re-read the current week's row every Monday. Use Section 4 to prepare each milestone review and Section 7 whenever something slips.

## 1. Program at a glance

### Goals

- **Day 30 (Wed 2026-11-18):** Both hires understand the two core topics: (1) customs and trade compliance fundamentals for the US, Canada and Mexico, and (2) trade systems and GTM (how trade data flows through the AS400 trade system, Oracle touchpoints, broker data exchange, e2open concepts). Both have worked through the analytics practice pack (at least exercises 1–6) and delivered one small real analysis.
- **Day 60 (Fri 2026-12-18):** Each hire is confident in their primary area (Research or Standard Cost), owns one custom tool, and is learning a second area (exports backlog items from week 6).
- **Day 90 (Fri 2027-01-15):** Each hire begins full ownership of their primary process: they run the cycle end to end as maker with a documented checker sign-off. The e2open as-is process doc and data map for their process are complete. They are picking up the other topics faster (reporting backlog from week 10, drawback exposure, other custom tools).

### The two tracks

### Boundary rule: Research owns the WHAT, Standard Cost owns the $

- **Research (Hire A)** owns the WHAT: the tariff or regulatory action, its legal scope, affected HTS codes and countries, rates, effective dates, the stacking and exclusion interpretation, and the sources. This is handed off in a **Tariff-Impact Brief** (`docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-brief.md`).
- **Standard Cost (Hire B)** owns the $: the impact on item landed cost and margin, the Oracle cost update, and the variance explanation.
- Both use one shared **Tariff-Impact Model** workbook (`dist/Tariff-Impact-Model-Template.xlsx`, guide in `docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-model-guide.md`).
- A brief that states dollar impacts, or a cost model that re-interprets legal scope, breaks the boundary. Reviewers send it back.

### Control rule: maker-checker

- Every standard cost update and every tariff model released to the business gets a documented second-person review by the Senior or the Manager before release.
- The checker records name, date, version reviewed and findings on the Jira ticket, and in the template's sign-off block where the template has one.
- This rule continues after day 90. "Ownership" means running the cycle, not approving it alone.
- Hire B never loads anything to [COMPANY: Oracle EBS or Fusion] production without Finance sign-off and a checker sign-off.

## 4. Milestone exit criteria

### How to use this section

- **Must** criteria decide the milestone outcome. **Stretch** criteria are tracked but never block "on track".
- Each hire collects evidence links (Jira, OneNote, SharePoint or file paths) in their copy of the scorecard. Evidence is due 2 working days before the review: Mon 11/16, Wed 12/16 and Wed 1/13.
- Ratings use the milestone rating in `docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md`: 1 = Not met, 2 = Partly met, 3 = Met, 4 = Exceeded. Proficiency targets inside criteria use the 0–4 proficiency scale: 0 = Not yet exposed, 1 = Aware (can explain the basics), 2 = Guided (does it with help), 3 = Independent (does it alone; work is reviewed), 4 = Owns/teaches (runs it, improves it, can train others).
- A "material error" is an error that would change a business decision: wrong items in scope, wrong rate, wrong effective date, wrong stacking result, or a cost or margin impact off by more than [COMPANY: materiality threshold].
- Criterion IDs (A30-1 and so on) are the same in the scorecards.

### Definition: "owns one tool" (day 60)

A hire owns a tool when all of the following are true. The tool is the one closest to their process, confirmed at the day-30 review: Hire A [COMPANY: tool closest to Research], Hire B [COMPANY: name of std-cost tool].

- [ ] Is the named first point of contact for the tool in the Tool Inventory tab and in OneNote.
- [ ] Runs it end to end without help, including the known failure modes and how to recover from them.
- [ ] Can explain its purpose, inputs, outputs, logic, data sources, schedule, dependencies (files, paths, credentials, Power Automate flows, linked tables) and who uses its output.
- [ ] Has published a tool doc (`docs/templates/knowledge/tool-doc.md`) that the Manager reviewed and could follow alone.
- [ ] Has confirmed where the source and a backup copy live, and has listed hard-coded values (rates, paths, dates) that create risk.
- [ ] Has made or triaged 1 or more changes through a change request (`docs/templates/bsa/06-change-request.md`), with a test, a checker review and a Jira trail.
- [ ] Keeps the tool's Jira backlog (known issues and enhancements) current.
- [ ] Has written the tool's e2open disposition note: replace, interface, retire or keep, with reasons.

Owning a tool does not mean approving your own changes. The Manager stays checker for tool changes.

## 6. Transitions and e2open

### Exports (M7): backlog items from W6

- **W5 prep:** The Senior runs the exports overview session: the team's role in exports, export classification (Schedule B, ECCN or EAR99), Electronic Export Information (EEI) filed through AES under the Foreign Trade Regulations (15 CFR Part 30), the Export Administration Regulations (15 CFR Parts 730–774), and awareness-level restricted party screening. The Senior and Manager pick 4–6 low-risk items in Jira (label: [COMPANY: Jira label for exports transition]).
- **Suitable items:** data corrections, master-data maintenance with review, report tweaks, documentation, reconciliations. **Not suitable inside 90 days:** licensing determinations, filing decisions, voluntary disclosures, anything with a regulatory deadline. These stay with the Senior.
- **W6 onward:** each hire takes 1 item at a time as maker. The Senior is checker and remains the owner of exports.
- **Targets:** 1 or more items per hire by day 60, 2 or more by day 90. Each completed item adds 1 or more Anki cards and a line in the exports SOP draft.

### Reporting (M8): backlog items from W10

- **W9 prep:** The Senior walks through the report inventory (sources, refresh schedule, consumers, known issues) and picks 1 low-risk item per hire (a Power BI fix, a new measure, a data warehouse view change).
- **W10–W13:** 1 item per hire as maker; Senior as checker; demo in W13.
- **Slip rule:** Reporting is the first thing to give if days 60–90 overload. Moving it past day 90 does not change the day-90 outcome.

### US duty drawback (M9): exposure only

- The Senior keeps drawback. The hires get exposure to it so their data work does not break it.
- **W11:** a 90-minute overview (moves to W13 if the Senior is out). It covers the main drawback types under 19 CFR Part 190 (manufacturing, unused merchandise, rejected merchandise); which import, export and inventory records a claim relies on; why export records from M7 matter; and why drawback on goods exported to Canada or Mexico is limited under USMCA Article 2.5 (19 CFR Part 182, Subpart E).
- **W12:** shadow the Senior on real drawback data preparation or review, with the Senior thinking aloud.
- **Output:** a 1-page note per hire, "what data drawback needs from our import and export records". It feeds the e2open data maps (A90-9, B90-9).

### e2open (M10) tie-in

- **W8–W9 prep:** the hires read the e2open sections of `docs/primers/trade-systems-gtm-e2open-primer.md`. The Manager walks through the as-is template (`docs/templates/bsa/03-process-map-as-is-to-be.md`) and the data-mapping template (`docs/templates/bsa/04-data-mapping-spec.md`). The Senior covers GTM data domains (product and classification, parties, trade agreements and origin, compliance, landed cost) against today's AS400 and warehouse data.
- **W10–W11 (holiday weeks):** solo drafting of the as-is doc and the data map for the hire's own process. This is good work for low-capacity weeks because it needs little trainer time and uses everything learned so far.
- **W12 kickoff:** the hires attend as core project BSAs and bring their drafts. They capture project scope, timeline, their role (as-is docs, data mapping and cleansing, then UAT) and open questions in OneNote.
- **W13:** the drafts are finalized and reviewed. These are the day-90 ownership evidence (A90-2, A90-3, B90-2, B90-3). Each owned tool's e2open disposition note (from the "owns one tool" definition) goes to the project team.
- **If kickoff moves:** the day-90 evidence does not depend on the kickoff date. If kickoff is later, the hires present their drafts at the kickoff. If earlier, they join earlier with the drafts in progress.

### Live tariff actions during training

New tariff actions are frequent. Use them as training material. They replace scheduled track sessions and never add trainer hours. The urgency rule depends on business days until the action takes effect:

- **Fewer than 5 business days, or business-critical:** the trainer is maker (Senior for the WHAT, Manager for the $). The hires shadow with think-aloud. Within 2 working days afterward, the hires write after-action notes: what changed, which SOP lines, Anki cards and model inputs need updating.
- **5–15 business days:** the hire is maker with a 15-minute daily checkpoint. Checker review before release, as always.
- **More than 15 business days:** the hire is maker on the normal cycle. The work replaces that week's scheduled track working session.
- A live action that meets the criterion counts as evidence for A60-1 and B60-1 (before day 60) or A90-1 and B90-1 (W10–W13).
- For current actions and sources, see `docs/primers/current-tariff-landscape.md`. Do not rely on rates or dates quoted in other docs.

### What gives first

When time runs short, drop work in this order. Record each decision in the plan change log (Section 10).

1. **Reporting (M8) backlog items:** slip past day 90.
2. **Drawback exposure (M9):** moves to days 91–120.
3. **Second tool doc and other custom tools:** move to days 91–120 (day-90 target becomes 1 tool doc).
4. **New exports items:** pause new items. Finish the item in progress. The minimum is 1 completed item per hire by day 90.
5. **Stretch criteria** in any milestone.

Never give:

- Maker-checker review.
- The primary-process cycle evidence (A90-1, B90-1).
- The e2open as-is doc and data map for the hire's own process.
- Weekly 1:1s. Shorten to 30 minutes if needed, but never skip 2 weeks in a row.

Trigger rules:

- If the as-is draft is under 50% at the end of W10, pause the reporting items (step 1).
- If the Senior has under 3 h available for 2 weeks running, the Manager becomes checker for Research briefs and drops M9 sessions (step 2).
- If a hire is rated "Extend" at day 60, apply steps 1–3 for that hire immediately.

## 8. Milestone reviews: what the Manager decides

### How each review runs

- **Two working days before:** the hire fills their scorecard in `docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md`, adding a self-rating and an evidence link for every criterion. The Senior sends ratings and notes for the criteria they checked (M1, M3, M7, M8, and Research briefs) in a 15-minute written input.
- **Prep (45 min per hire):** the Manager opens every evidence link, spot-checks 10 Anki cards, verifies 3 Skills-tab ratings against work products, and pre-fills ratings.
- **The review (60 min per hire):** the agenda is in `docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md`. It ends with a decision and the next-30-days plan.
- **Within 1 working day:** the Manager records the decision and changes in the scorecard and in the plan change log (Section 10), and tells the Senior.

### Decision rules

| Outcome | When | What happens next |
|---|----------|------------------|
| On track | Every Must criterion rated 3 or 4, and no criterion rated 1 | Proceed as planned. Confirm the next milestone's evidence items, e.g. which tariff action or cost cycle. |
| Adjust | No Must criterion rated 1, but one or two Must criteria rated 2; or any Stretch rated 1 | Up to 3 written recovery actions, each with an owner and a due date within 2 weeks, checked in 1:1s. The next phase starts on time with scope reduced using "What gives first". |
| Extend | Any Must criterion rated 1, or three or more Must criteria rated 2 | Extend this milestone for that hire. Re-check only the unmet Must criteria on the re-check date below. Shift the hire's next-phase targets and apply "What gives first". |

Re-check dates if extended: day 30 is re-checked Wed 2026-12-02 (W7), and that hire's exports start moves to W8. Day 60 is re-checked in the W12 1:1 (Fri 2027-01-08), with day-90 scope reduced using steps 1–3. Day 90 is re-checked Fri 2027-02-12; until then the trainer stays maker for production cycles and the hire co-makes.

If the concern is about conduct or effort rather than learning pace, follow [COMPANY: HR performance process] in addition to this plan.

### Decisions specific to each milestone

- **Day 30:** confirm the tool each hire will own by day 60. Confirm the exports items for W6. Decide whether Hire B's data depth stretch (B60-8) is realistic. Confirm the Finance contact time for Hire B.
- **Day 60:** confirm the day-90 evidence: which live tariff action for Hire A (or the rule for picking the next one), and which cost cycle option for Hire B (live, parallel run or interim update). Decide whether reporting items start in W10 or slip. Confirm e2open kickoff date and the hires' project roles.
- **Day 90:** decide the ownership start date (default Mon 2027-01-18) and the checker for each process going forward. Approve the days 91–180 plan: reporting ownership path, e2open data cleansing and UAT, remaining custom tools, drawback depth.
