# 30-60-90 Day Plan: BSA New Hires

Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Owner: BSA Manager

**Purpose:** The master plan for the first 90 days of two early-career Business Systems Analysts (BSAs). It covers goals, week-by-week roadmap, milestone exit criteria, trainer load and the standard weekly timetable, transitions, risks and milestone decisions.

**Who uses it:** BSA Manager (owner), Senior BSA (co-trainer), Hire A (Research track), Hire B (Standard Cost track).

**When:** Manager and Senior: read in full before Day 1 (Mon 2026-10-19). Hires: walked through it in the Day-1 welcome (Mon 10/19 13:00); read Sections 1 and 3 starting in the Day-1 16:00 block, finished by Fri 10/23 (the Friday 16:00 catch-up buffer). Everyone: re-read the current week's row every Monday. Use Section 4 to prepare each milestone review and Section 7 whenever something slips. Terms and acronyms are in the Glossary tab of the trackers workbook.

### Boundary rule: Research owns the WHAT, Standard Cost owns the $

- **Research (Hire A)** owns the WHAT: the tariff or regulatory action, its legal scope, which of our items are in scope, affected HTS codes and countries, rates, effective dates, the stacking and exclusion interpretation, and the sources. This is handed off in a **Tariff-Impact Brief** (`docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-brief.md`).
- **Standard Cost (Hire B)** owns the $: the impact on item landed cost and margin, the Oracle cost update, and the variance explanation. Standard Cost never decides scope; it computes the $ on Research's determinations.
- Both use one shared **Tariff-Impact Model** workbook (Tariff-Impact-Model-Template.xlsx in the onboarding folder; guide in `docs/templates/domain/tariff-impact-model-guide.md`).
- A brief that states dollar impacts, or a cost model that re-interprets legal scope, breaks the boundary. Reviewers send it back.

### Control rule: maker-checker

- Every standard cost update and every tariff model released to the business gets a documented second-person review by the Senior or the Manager before release.
- The checker records name, date, version reviewed and findings on the Jira ticket, and in the template's sign-off block where the template has one.
- This rule continues after day 90. "Ownership" means running the cycle, not approving it alone.
- Hire B never loads anything to Oracle EBS production without Finance sign-off and a checker sign-off.
- **Brokers and counsel:** before the day-60 review the hires never contact a customs broker, agente aduanal or trade counsel directly. Questions go through the Senior (or the Manager). After day 60, with the Senior's agreement, a hire may email the broker with the Senior copied.

## 4. Milestone exit criteria

### How to use this section

- Each criterion is **Must**, **Should** or **Stretch**. **Should** criteria are expected: a Should rated 1 or 2 gets a catch-up action in the next 1:1, but does not change the milestone outcome. Only Must criteria decide it; Stretch never blocks "on track".
- Each hire collects evidence links (Jira, OneNote, SharePoint or file paths) in their copy of the scorecard. Evidence is due 2 working days before the review: Mon 11/16, Wed 12/16 and Wed 1/13.
- Ratings use the milestone rating in `docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md`: 1 = Not met, 2 = Partly met, 3 = Met, 4 = Exceeded. Proficiency targets inside criteria use the 0–4 proficiency scale: 0 = Not yet exposed, 1 = Aware (can explain the basics), 2 = Guided (does it with help), 3 = Independent (does it alone; work is reviewed), 4 = Owns/teaches (runs it, improves it, can train others).
- A "material error" is an error that would change a business decision: wrong items in scope, wrong rate, wrong effective date, wrong stacking result, or a cost or margin impact off by more than [COMPANY: materiality threshold].
- Criterion IDs (A30-1 and so on) are the same in the scorecards. The schedule, owner, input and fallback for every Must criterion are in "Must criteria: delivery plan" at the end of this section.

## 6. Transitions and e2open

### Exports (M7): backlog items from W6

- **W5 prep:** the Senior's exports overview covers the team's role, export classification (Schedule B, ECCN or EAR99), EEI filed through AES under 15 CFR Part 30, the EAR (15 CFR Parts 730–774) and awareness-level restricted party screening. The Senior and Manager pick 4–6 low-risk items in Jira ([COMPANY: Jira label for exports transition]).
- **Suitable items:** data corrections, master-data maintenance with review, report tweaks, documentation, reconciliations. **Not suitable inside 90 days:** licensing determinations, filing decisions, voluntary disclosures, anything with a regulatory deadline. These stay with the Senior.
- **W6 onward:** each hire takes 1 item at a time as maker: item 1 in W6–W7, item 2 in W8–W10. The Senior is checker and remains the owner of exports.
- **Targets:** 1 or more items per hire by day 60, 2 or more by day 90. Each completed item adds Anki cards and a line in the exports SOP draft.

### Reporting (M8): backlog items from W10

- **W9 prep:** the Senior walks through the report inventory (sources, refresh, consumers, known issues) and picks 1 low-risk item per hire (a Power BI fix, a new measure, a warehouse view change).
- **W10–W13:** 1 item per hire as maker, started when that hire's exports item 2 is done (one transition item at a time); Senior as checker; demo in W13.
- **Slip rule:** Reporting is Stretch work (A90-8, B90-8) and the first transition work to give if days 60–90 overload. Moving it past day 90 does not change the day-90 outcome.

### US duty drawback (M9): exposure only

- The Senior keeps drawback. The hires get exposure so their data work does not break it.
- **W11:** a 90-minute overview: the main drawback types under 19 CFR Part 190 (manufacturing, unused merchandise, rejected merchandise), the import, export and inventory records a claim relies on, and why drawback on goods exported to Canada or Mexico is limited under USMCA Article 2.5 (19 CFR Part 182, Subpart E).

### e2open (M10) tie-in

### Live tariff actions during training

New tariff actions are frequent. Use them as training material. They replace scheduled track sessions and never add trainer hours. The urgency rule depends on business days until the action takes effect:

- **Fewer than 5 business days, or business-critical:** the trainer is maker (Senior for the WHAT, Manager for the $) and the other trainer is checker; record both on the Jira ticket. The hires shadow with think-aloud and draft a parallel practice brief or model, reviewed afterward. Within 2 working days, they write after-action notes: what changed, which SOP lines, Anki cards and model inputs need updating. In W1–W2 the trainer is always maker.
- **5–15 business days:** the hire is maker, with a 15-minute daily checkpoint. The checker reviews each draft on the same business day it is submitted (the time comes from the checker's review or buffer line in Section 5), and nothing is released before that review.
- **More than 15 business days:** the hire is maker on the normal cycle. The work replaces that week's scheduled track working session.
- A live action with 5 or more business days' lead time counts as evidence for A60-1 and B60-1 (before day 60) or A90-1 and B90-1 (W10–W13).
- For current actions and sources, see `docs/primers/current-tariff-landscape.md`. Do not rely on rates or dates quoted in other docs.

### What gives first

When time runs short, drop work in this order. Stretch work goes before anything a Must criterion depends on. Record each decision in the plan change log (Section 10); the scorecard then rates against the reduced target.

1. **Other Stretch work in the current milestone** (A30-10, B30-10, A60-8, B60-8).
2. **Reporting (M8) backlog items** (Stretch A90-8, B90-8): slip past day 90.
3. **Drawback exposure (M9)** (Stretch A90-9, B90-9): moves to days 91–120.
4. **Second tool doc and other custom tools:** move to days 91–120 (the A90-7 and B90-7 target becomes 1 tool doc).
5. **New exports items:** pause new items and finish the item in progress (the A90-5 and B90-5 target becomes 1 completed item).

Never give:

- Maker-checker review.
- The primary-process cycle evidence (A90-1, B90-1).
- The e2open as-is doc and data map for the hire's own process.
- Weekly 1:1s. Shorten to 30 minutes if needed, but never skip 2 weeks in a row.

Trigger rules:

- If the as-is draft is under 50% at the end of W10, pause the reporting items (step 2).
- If the Senior has under 3 h available for 2 weeks running, the Manager becomes checker for Research briefs and drops M9 sessions (step 3).
- If a hire is rated "Extend" at day 60, apply steps 1–3 for that hire immediately.

## 8. Milestone reviews: what the Manager decides

### How each review runs

- **Two working days before:** the hire fills their scorecard (`docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md`) with a self-rating and an evidence link per criterion. The Senior sends a 15-minute written input on the criteria they checked (M1, M3, M7, M8, the SSMS lab, Research briefs).
- **Prep (30 min per hire at day 30 and day 60, 45 min at day 90) and the 60-minute review:** as in `docs/03-check-ins-and-scorecards.md` Section 6. The review ends with a decision and the next-30-days plan.
- **Within 1 working day:** the Manager records the decision in the scorecard and the plan change log (Section 10), and tells the Senior.

### Decision rules

| Outcome | When | What happens next |
|---|----------|------------------|
| On track | Every Must criterion rated 3 or 4. Should and Stretch ratings are recorded but do not change the outcome; a Should rated 1 or 2 gets a catch-up action in the next 1:1 (Section 4). | Proceed as planned. Confirm the next milestone's evidence items, e.g. which tariff action or cost cycle. |
| Adjust | No Must criterion rated 1, but one or two Must criteria rated 2 | Up to 3 written recovery actions, each with an owner and a due date within 2 weeks, checked in 1:1s. The next phase starts on time with scope reduced using "What gives first". |
| Extend | Any Must criterion rated 1, or three or more Must criteria rated 2 | Extend this milestone for that hire. Re-check only the unmet Must criteria on the re-check date below. Shift the hire's next-phase targets and apply "What gives first". |

Re-check dates if extended: day 30 is re-checked Wed 2026-12-02 (W7), and that hire's exports start moves to W8. Day 60 is re-checked in the W12 1:1 (Fri 2027-01-08), with day-90 scope reduced using steps 1–3. Day 90 is re-checked Fri 2027-02-12; until then the trainer stays maker for production cycles and the hire co-makes.

If the concern is about conduct or effort rather than learning pace, follow [COMPANY: HR performance process] in addition to this plan.

- **Day 30:** confirm the tool each hire will own by day 60 and request its test copy (AR-12, AR-13, AR-15). Confirm the exports items for W6, the action for A60-1 and the ad-hoc investigation for A60-3. Decide whether Hire B's data depth stretch (B60-8) is realistic.
